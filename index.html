One-week Individual Programme

Everything folded into a single week

Five elements, one homestay — designed so language, culture and daily life happen at the same time, not in separate boxes.

01 · Tuition

Private lessons

10 hours a week of one-to-one, tailored Mandarin — General Chinese, Business Chinese, or HSK exam preparation.

02 · Outdoor

Outdoor sessions

Several hours each week move outside the classroom, applying everyday Mandarin in real situations with your teacher.

03 · Homestay

Host-family stay

A full week living in your teacher's home, breakfast included, with language and culture woven into daily life.

04 · Culture

Cultural activity

Pick one hands-on activity — calligraphy, an instrument, traditional arts, or a cooking class.

05 · Excursion

Day trip

Choose sightseeing or a local festival — a full day exploring the city and region beyond the classroom.
